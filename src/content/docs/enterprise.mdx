Enterprise is the same canvas and API as every other plan. These controls are for larger teams:

- [SSO](/enterprise/saml/) — SAML 2.0 or OIDC, verified login domains, just-in-time provisioning, and Require SSO. Included on Business and Enterprise.
- [Team & permissions](/security/team-permissions/) — built-in and custom roles, teams, and project ownership
- [Audit logs](/enterprise/audit-logs/)
- [Two-factor authentication](/security/two-factor-auth/) — authenticator apps, passkeys, and recovery codes
- [Inbound IP rules](/security/ip-allowlist/) — allow or block source IPs per service (Business)
- Support plans up to Enterprise, with 24/7 response and an uptime SLA — [Pricing](/reference/pricing/#support-plans)

For an Enterprise agreement, talk to sales at [stackblaze.com/contact](https://stackblaze.com/contact).
