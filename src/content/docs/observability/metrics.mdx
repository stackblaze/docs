---
title: Metrics
description: CPU, memory, and HTTP metrics on the service, and Coroot.
---

The service's **Metrics** tab shows **Resources** (CPU and memory) and **HTTP** (request rate and response time) over 2 hours, 24 hours, or 7 days. Deeper metrics, traces, and the service map are in **Coroot**: click **Open in Coroot** (one-time handoff).

```bash
stackblaze metrics               # current CPU and memory
stackblaze metrics --since 24h   # history, up to 7d
```

See [Observability](/operations/observability/).
