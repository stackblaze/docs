---
title: Logs
description: Runtime logs on the service, search, log drains, and the CLI.
---

Every service has a **Logs** tab: stdout/stderr from the running revision, with a search box. It also links to the build logs and system events. A deploy's build and rollout logs open from **Inspect Deployment** on the **Deployment** tab.

Searching history uses the durable log store (the Logs tab, or `stackblaze logs --search`). An empty live tail is not the same as “no logs”: the instance may have restarted.

```bash
stackblaze logs
stackblaze logs -f
stackblaze logs --search -q error --minutes 60
```

Retention follows your [plan](/reference/pricing/) (shown under the service's **Settings → Logs**). To keep logs longer, forward them with a log drain under **Settings → Log drains**. Metrics and traces: [Observability](/operations/observability/).
