A **project** is the unit you compose infrastructure in. Open it and you land on the [canvas](/concepts/#the-canvas).

## What a project contains

- [Services](/build-deploy/apps/) — Git, Docker, cron, functions, templates
- [Databases](/databases/) — managed add-ons on the same board
- [Environments](/deploy/environments/) — production, custom, PR previews
- [Variables](/security/env-vars/) — per service, per environment

The project runs in the region you pick when you create it. The default comes from the organization's **Default region for new projects** (**Settings → General**).

## Members

People join the organization, not a single project. Invite them from **Settings → Access control**. Their organization role (built-in `admin`, `member`, `guest`, or a custom role) decides what they can do. See [Team & permissions](/security/team-permissions/).

To limit a project to one team, set its **Owning team** in **Project settings → Ownership & access**. Members of that team get their team role on the project. Organization admins and members with organization-wide roles always have access.

## Usage

The dashboard's **Usage** and **Billing** pages show what the organization uses and pays. Plan limits (projects, services per project, environments per project) are on [pricing](/reference/pricing/) and [limits](/reference/limits/).

## Delete

Deleting a project removes its environments, services, and databases. That cannot be undone from the dashboard.
