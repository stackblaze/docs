A **project** is the unit you compose infrastructure in. Open it and you land on the [canvas](/concepts/#the-canvas).

## What a project contains

- [Services](/build-deploy/apps/) — Git, Docker, cron, templates
- [Databases](/databases/) — managed add-ons on the same board
- [Environments](/deploy/environments/) — production, custom, PR previews
- [Variables](/security/env-vars/) — per service, per environment

The project is pinned to a region at create time.

## Members

Invite people from project or organization settings. Roles are [Owner, Admin, Developer, Viewer](/security/team-permissions/).

## Usage

Plan limits and billing are on [pricing](/reference/pricing/) and [limits](/reference/limits/).

## Delete

Deleting a project removes its environments, services, and databases. That is irreversible from the dashboard.
