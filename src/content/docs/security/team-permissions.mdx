Access is set per **organization**. You invite people to the organization and give each one a role. Teams group members, and each project has an owning team.

Manage all of this in **Settings → Access control**. It has four tabs: **Users**, **Invitations**, **Teams**, and **Roles**.

## Roles

Every organization has three built-in roles. They cannot be changed or deleted.

| Role | What it can do |
| --- | --- |
| **admin** | Full access within the organization: services, projects, members, organization settings, tokens, security, audit log, console, logs, and restarts. |
| **member** | Create, deploy, and manage services and projects. Use the console, logs, and restarts. Read the member list and the audit log. Cannot change organization settings. |
| **guest** | Read-only access to services and projects, the audit log, and security settings. No console, no logs, no restarts, no tokens. |

### Custom roles

On the **Roles** tab, click **Create role** to define your own. A role sets a level for each area:

- **Apps**, **Projects**, **Accounts**, **Settings**, **Security**, **Tokens** — none, read, or write
- **Audit**, **Console**, **Logs**, **Reboot** — on or off

Role changes apply on the member's next request. No redeploy is needed.

## The organization owner

Each organization has one owner. The owner can transfer ownership to another **admin** from the **Users** tab (**Transfer ownership…**). The other person must accept.

## Invite a member

1. Open **Settings → Access control** and open the invite form.
2. Enter one or more email addresses.
3. Choose a role under **Assign organization role**.
4. Optionally pick a team under **Add to a team**.
5. Click **Invite**.

Invitations expire after 7 days. Pending invitations are on the **Invitations** tab, where you can revoke them. Pending invitations count toward your plan's [member limit](/reference/limits/).

## Teams

A team is a named group of members. On the **Teams** tab you create teams and add members, each with a role inside that team.

Each project has an **owning team**. Set it in **Project settings → Ownership & access**. Members of that team get their team role on the project. Organization admins and members with organization-wide roles always have access.

## Remove a member

On the **Users** tab, choose **Remove from organization** for the member. Their access to the organization ends at once.

## Single sign-on

On Business plans and Enterprise agreements you can sign members in through your identity provider and give new members a default role. See [SSO](/enterprise/saml/).
