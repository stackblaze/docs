---
title: Limits & Quotas
description: Per-plan limits for projects, services, databases, environments, members, instances, service size, and builds.
---

Each plan caps how much you can create. When you reach a cap, the dashboard, CLI, and API refuse the change that would go over it (HTTP 402 with an upgrade hint). What is already running keeps running.

## Per-plan limits

| Limit | Free Trial | Developer | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Projects | 1 | 5 | 25 | 100 |
| Services per project | 3 | 10 | 50 | 200 |
| Environments per project | 1 | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| Databases (Postgres, MariaDB, Redis — one count per organization) | 2 | 2 | 10 | 30 |
| Regions in use | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| Team members (including pending invites) | 1 | 1 | 5 | 20 |
| Max instances per service | 1 | 1 | 3 | 10 |
| Max size per service | 0.5 vCPU · 0.5 GB | 2 vCPU · 4 GB | 4 vCPU · 16 GB | 8 vCPU · 32 GB |
| Database connections | 100 | 200 | 400 | 500 |
| Redis memory | 25 MB | Up to max size | Up to max size | Up to max size |
| Redis connections | 50 | 200 | 1,000 | 2,000 |
| Concurrent builds | 1 | 2 | 5 | 10 |
| Build timeout | 10 min | 30 min | 60 min | 90 min |
| Database backup retention | Not available | 7 days | 14 days | 30 days |
| Log retention | Not promised | 3 days | 14 days | 30 days |

## What counts

- **Services per project** — every service on the canvas, including each service a template adds (an n8n template is three). Databases do not take a service slot.
- **Databases** — Postgres, MariaDB, and Redis count together, across all projects in the organization. Removing one frees the slot at once.
- **Max size per service** — the total for all instances of a service. Two instances share the cap.
- **Concurrent builds** — a build over the limit is refused. There is no queue; a push that arrives while you are at the limit is skipped.
- **Builds** — free and unlimited. There are no build minutes.

## When you hit a limit

Upgrade from **Billing** in the dashboard. After a downgrade, anything above the new limits keeps running, but you cannot add more until you are back under.
