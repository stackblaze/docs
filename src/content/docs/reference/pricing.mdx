## How the bill works

On a paid plan the price is a monthly minimum that counts toward usage. It is not a fee on top. You pay the greater of the plan price or your metered usage.

## Usage rates

The same on every plan.

| Resource | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| CPU | $14 per vCPU-month, metered on actual use |
| Memory | $7 per GB-month, metered on actual use |
| Persistent storage | $0.15 per provisioned GB-month |
| Database backups | First 10 GB free, then $0.15 per GB-month stored |
| Outbound traffic | $0.05 per GB (inbound is free) |
| Email sent from your apps | $1 per 1,000 emails |
| Builds | Free |

A month is 730 hours. Storage is billed on the provisioned size of a volume, not on what it holds.

## Changing plans

Change your plan from **Billing** in the dashboard. Usage for the current period is on **Usage**.

- **Upgrade** — the new limits apply at once. A running service keeps its size until its next save, deploy, or restart.
- **Downgrade** — takes effect at the end of the billing period. Services above the new caps are scaled down then. Anything you have more of than the new plan includes (databases, environments, members, regions) keeps running, but you cannot add more until you are back under the limit.
- **Cancel** — you keep the plan until the paid period ends and are not charged again.
