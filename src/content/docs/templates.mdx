---
title: Templates
description: One-click stacks that land on the project canvas.
---

Templates are pre-wired groups of services and databases, such as n8n or Outline. Deploy from the [template catalog](https://stackblaze.com/templates) or from **+ Service → Template** on an existing canvas.

Fill in any prompted values, then [deploy staged changes](/deploy/staged-changes/). The canvas provisions every service the template defines.

You can also save part of a live environment as a stack and deploy it again later. See [Create a template](/templates/create/).

To describe a stack in git instead, use [config as code](/blueprint/).
