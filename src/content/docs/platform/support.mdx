- **Docs**: this site. Search with `Ctrl K` / `⌘ K`.
- **Dashboard**: build logs, runtime logs, and metrics on the service.
- **Support tickets**: open **Support** in the dashboard sidebar (or the help menu in the header) to open a ticket and follow the replies. We reply there and by email.
- **Community**: the Discord server, linked from the dashboard help menu.

Include the **project**, **environment**, **service**, and a deployment id when you write in. Do not send kubectl output. We cannot use it, and you do not need cluster access.

## Support plans

Community support comes with every plan. Standard and Premium are add-ons you can buy on any plan.

| Plan | Price | First response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Community | Free, with every plan | Best effort |

| Premium | $149 / month | 8 hours, 1 hour for urgent issues |
| Enterprise | [Contact sales](https://stackblaze.com/contact) | 24/7 with an uptime SLA |
