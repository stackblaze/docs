---
title: Audit logs
description: Who changed a project, when, and from where.
---

Enterprise organizations retain an audit log of admin actions: member invites, role changes, SSO, IP allowlist edits, and destructive canvas operations. Entries include timestamp, actor, and source IP.

In the dashboard, open organization settings → **Audit**.

From the CLI:

```bash
stackblaze audit
```

Retention is 90 days on Enterprise. This is not a substitute for application logs — those live in [Observability](/operations/observability/).
