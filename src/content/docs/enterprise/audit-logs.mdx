---
title: Audit logs
description: Who changed what in your organization, and when.
---

The audit log records changes across your organization: who made them, what changed, and when.

Open **Audit log** in the dashboard. You can search by user, action, resource, or service, filter by severity, and pick a time range. One query covers at most 90 days.

Reading the audit log needs the **Audit** permission. All built-in roles (admin, member, guest) have it. See [Team & permissions](/security/team-permissions/).

From the CLI:

```bash
stackblaze audit
stackblaze audit --actor alice --action delete --since-minutes 60
```

Revealing secret variable values on a service is also recorded.

The audit log is not a substitute for application logs. Those live in [Observability](/operations/observability/).
