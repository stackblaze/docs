---
title: stackblaze list
description: "List pipelines and their phases. Alias of pipelines list."
---

Same as [`stackblaze pipelines list`](/cli/pipelines/). Alias: `stackblaze ls`.

## Usage

```text
stackblaze list [options]
```

Also accepts the [scope and output flags](/cli/#scope-and-common-flags): `-p, --pipeline`, `--phase`, `-a, --app`, `--token`, `--api-url`, `-o, --output`, `--json`.
