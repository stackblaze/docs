---
title: stackblaze list
description: "List projects and their environments. Alias of projects list."
---

Same as [`stackblaze projects list`](/cli/projects/). Alias: `stackblaze ls`.

## Usage

```text
stackblaze list [options]
```

Also accepts the [scope and output flags](/cli/#scope-and-common-flags): `-p, --project`, `-e, --env`, `-a, --app`, `--token`, `--api-url`, `-o, --output`, `--json`.
