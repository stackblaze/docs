Add a database from **+ Service → Databases**. Kafka, RabbitMQ, Memcached, and object storage are under **+ Service → Add-ons**. Each lands on the canvas as a staged change, joins the environment's private network, and gives the services you connect it to their connection [variables](/security/env-vars/).

## Databases

Every database starts as a single instance. PostgreSQL, MariaDB, Valkey, FerretDB, and OpenSearch can switch to high availability later from **Settings → HA**.
