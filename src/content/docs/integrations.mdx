Organization accounts on other services are under **Settings → Integrations**: the StackBlaze **GitHub** App, which deploys your repositories, and **Hugging Face**, which downloads gated AI models.

There is no OAuth app platform for third-party StackBlaze apps, and no GraphQL endpoint. Use REST. Interactive OpenAPI lives at [api.stackblaze.cloud/api/docs](https://api.stackblaze.cloud/api/docs).
