title: SSL / TLS
description: Automatic HTTPS on StackBlaze URLs and custom domains, with certificates issued and renewed for you.

Every public StackBlaze service gets automatic HTTPS. Certificates for custom domains are issued and renewed without any configuration.

## Automatic HTTPS

A service's default URL under `stackblaze.app` is served over HTTPS from its first deploy. StackBlaze manages that certificate; you never need to think about it. In the service settings the default URL shows **HTTPS included**.

## Custom domain certificates

When you add a [custom domain](/networking/custom-domains/), StackBlaze issues a certificate for it after DNS verification: the TXT ownership record and the CNAME must both resolve. The certificate is a domain-validated (DV) certificate from **Let's Encrypt**, trusted by all major browsers and operating systems.

Issuing usually takes a few minutes after verification. The domain's DNS dialog shows "Verified — issuing the HTTPS certificate" and then "Verified — HTTPS certificate issued. This domain is live." Renewal is automatic.

Because every new hostname needs a certificate, a workspace can add at most 20 new hostnames per 24 hours.

There is no upload for your own certificates.

## Where TLS ends

TLS is terminated at the StackBlaze edge in front of your service. Your container receives plain HTTP on its port. Do not terminate TLS in the container.

## Verifying your SSL configuration

Use `curl` or SSL Labs to check a domain after it goes live:

```bash title="Terminal"
# Check TLS version and certificate
curl -vI https://app.example.com 2>&1 | grep -E "SSL|TLS|certificate|issuer"

# Full SSL analysis
open https://www.ssllabs.com/ssltest/analyze.html?d=app.example.com
```

## Troubleshooting

- **The certificate is not issued**: check that both DNS records resolve (see [verification](/networking/custom-domains/#verification)) and that no CAA record excludes Let's Encrypt.
- **The browser shows a certificate error right after adding a domain**: wait until the DNS dialog says the certificate is issued.
